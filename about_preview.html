About

A data scientist that fits on your laptop.

AutoDS takes the work that normally needs a specialist and a lot of time, and does it for you in minutes, on your own machine, with the reasoning shown at every step.

What it is

A no-code assistant for the whole workflow.

Most useful questions are hiding in a spreadsheet, who is likely to leave, what next month looks like, which reviews are unhappy. Answering them normally means knowing Python and machine learning, and doing a lot of repetitive work. AutoDS runs that whole workflow, from exploring the data to a trained model and a written analysis, and it explains the result so someone without a data background can act on it.

Who it is for

Anyone with data and a question.

Analysts who want a faster first pass, small business owners without a data team, students learning the craft, product and operations people who need an answer this week, and researchers exploring a dataset. If you have a spreadsheet and a question, it is for you.

Where it is used

Real questions, across industries.

Why it exists

To take the grind off the person.

A lot of data science is the same routine every time. Load, clean, chart, pick a model, score it, explain it. That routine is slow, easy to get wrong, and usually locked behind code. AutoDS does the routine carefully and safely, checks the data for the traps that quietly ruin a model, and hands back not just a number but a story you can trust and share. Your time goes to the questions that matter.

How it stays honest

Careful by design.

It keeps a portion of your data unseen and reports the score on that. It checks for leakage and other structural traps before modelling. It cross checks the winning model across several splits. And it runs entirely on your machine, so your data never leaves your computer.
